## Blocker reported to root

`CookingRecipeSupply.cs:ValidateSupplyCheckpoint` validates receipt/origin/provenance associations but does not validate `checkpoint.NextProductId` against created supply objects. Ordinary `CookingRecipeCheckpoint.cs:ValidateCheckpoint` only checks it is nonnegative. A valid received delivery creates one package plus N raw identities, including tombstones after processing. Setting only NextProductId=0 preserves every supply association, ordinary item invariant and event history, passes the visible validators and is installed. The next Receive reuses allocator sequence1 and hits existing identities. Allocation safely rejects without duplicate stock, but this accepts an invalid recovery watermark and can permanently block future materialization. This is a static-path finding, not a newly executed test. Root was notified before source changes; no self-fix was attempted.

Minimum defensible fix: check a conservative monotonic lower bound counting all original supply-created objects (including removed instances) plus independently created products retained as registry/tombstones. This catches underflow without assuming custom ID formatting or invoking a stateful allocator during validation. Exact arbitrary allocator sequence validation would require recorded allocation-sequence provenance, not inferring sequence from arbitrary ItemId strings. Add poison restore asserting rejection and exact no-mutation, and legal consumed/tombstone restore control. Independent root decision remains required.
